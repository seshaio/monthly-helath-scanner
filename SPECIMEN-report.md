**How the score works.** Health 0–4 + valuation vs its own 5-year history 0–4
+ trend 0–2. **8–10 BUY · 4–7 KEEP · 0–3 SELL.**

Two overrides sit on top of the band:
**TRIM** — business healthy but 4+ of its 5 valuation anchors at the 85th
percentile or above. Reduce, don't exit. **WAIT** — reports within 5 days.
A broken business is always SELL regardless of how cheap it looks.

Thresholds are fixed in `config.py` before the data is fetched, so the number
can't be talked into a comfortable answer.
