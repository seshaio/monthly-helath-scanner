### Yield anchors that have gone negative

A yield anchor ranks *low value = dear*. That inverts correctly while the yield is positive. Below zero it does not: there is no yield left to be dear or cheap on, so the rank stops describing the price and starts describing the business — while still being counted as valuation. Read these names on the figures, not the percentile.
